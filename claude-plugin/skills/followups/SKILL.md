# Follow-ups with Co-op Coach

1. Call `daily_brief` (days=7) and `my_applications`.
2. Summarize: new inbox updates, OAs/interviews in progress (`prep_for`), and `quiet_3_weeks`.
3. For quiet applications worth chasing (startups, roles they care about), draft a short, polite follow-up (3-5
   sentences, specific to the role, no invented facts). Big-company portal applications rarely need one: say so.
4. Offer `/coop-coach:interview-prep` for anything in OA/interview.
5. If they tell you an outcome, update it with `track` (oa, interviewing, offer, rejected).

Drafts only: never send email or messages on their behalf.
