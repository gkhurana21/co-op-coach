Connect your app

In Co-op Coach, go to Settings → AI & extension → Chrome extension and press Get a code. Paste it here.

The code works for 5 minutes. Restarted Chrome or the app? Get a new one.

Fill an application

On a job application page? Press the button: it fills in what it can and attaches your resume. Check everything, then press Submit yourself.

Read my Outlook

Only needed if Microsoft sign-in doesn't work for your school. Open Outlook in this tab and press the button: it reads your job emails while you watch. It never sends or deletes anything.
